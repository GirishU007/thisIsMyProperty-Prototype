To collect and analyze real home product performance data and use it to create a safer, more transparent, and more competitive home services industry.

Expose Planned Obsolescence

Identify products that are designed to fail early so consumers can make smarter choices.

Prevent Price Gouging

Detect and report unfair pricing by contractors, especially targeting vulnerable populations and specific zip codes.

Improve Products

Sell anonymized failure reports to manufacturers and their competitors so they can build better, longer-lasting products.

Give Back to Homeowners

Fund local businesses that help elderly and disabled homeowners, and neighbors recovering from natural disasters.
